# Product Discovery

## Overview

This folder contains the initial Product Discovery documentation for **Cubiland**, a gamified speedcubing learning and training platform.

The documents describe the target audience and problem space, product positioning, competitor landscape, proposed MVP scope, feature priorities, and assumptions that require validation.

These documents establish the product direction and provide input for Requirements Engineering and subsequent design and development phases.

## Documents

| Document | Description |
|---|---|
| `TARGET_AUDIENCE.md` | Defines the target market, audience segments, user needs, problem analysis, illustrative personas, and user research plan. |
| `COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md` | Reviews direct and adjacent competitors, compares publicly described capabilities, and identifies areas for further evaluation. |
| `VALUE_PROPOSITION.md` | Describes Cubiland's product value, positioning, user benefits, differentiation direction, and validation questions. |
| `MVP_DEFINITION.md` | Defines the proposed first-release scope, core user journey, initial course scope, MVP capabilities, and unresolved scope decisions. |
| `FEATURE_PRIORITIZATION.md` | Records the feature inventory, priority levels, MVP inclusion, Free/Premium notes, and open product decisions. |
| `PRODUCT_HYPOTHESES.md` | Documents the core product hypotheses and the proposed methods for validating them through interviews, competitor evaluation, prototype testing, and monetization research. |

## Recommended Review Order

1. `TARGET_AUDIENCE.md` — understand who Cubiland is intended to serve and which problems the product addresses.
2. `COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md` — understand the existing product landscape and relevant benchmarks.
3. `VALUE_PROPOSITION.md` — review the intended product value and positioning.
4. `MVP_DEFINITION.md` — understand the proposed initial release and core user journey.
5. `FEATURE_PRIORITIZATION.md` — review feature priorities, MVP status, and Free/Premium notes.
6. `PRODUCT_HYPOTHESES.md` — identify assumptions and how they should be validated.

## Document Status and Usage

- These documents represent the initial Product Discovery baseline.
- Competitor findings are based on desk research.
- Product hypotheses are assumptions until supported by research evidence.
- Open decisions and `TBD` values must remain unresolved until an explicit product decision is made.
- Priority, MVP inclusion, and Free/Premium availability are separate attributes; one must not be inferred from another.
- When a product decision changes, update the relevant source document and keep dependent requirements and design artefacts consistent.

## Relationship to Other Phases

Product Discovery provides the foundation for:

- **Requirements Engineering:** translating the product direction, feature scope, and hypotheses into requirements and business rules.
- **UX/UI Design:** informing information architecture, user flows, wireframes, and interface design.
- **Technical Architecture:** providing product capabilities and constraints that guide system design.
