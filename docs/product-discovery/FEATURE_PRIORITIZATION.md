## 1. Purpose and Prioritization Model

This document records the planned Cubiland feature set and its current priority, MVP inclusion, and Free/Premium treatment.

The priority levels are:

- **Must-have** — considered essential to the intended product experience.
- **Should-have** — important to the product, but not necessarily required for the first release.
- **Nice-to-have** — adds value and may be delivered after the core experience is established.

Priority, MVP inclusion, and Premium availability are separate attributes. A feature can be Must-have while its MVP status or subscription treatment remains undecided.

The table preserves the current product decisions and original prioritization. Unresolved items are recorded as open product decisions rather than silently assigned a value.

## 2. Feature Inventory

### 2.1 User Profile

- **Description:** User registration, authentication, and profile functionality. Users can sign in and retain their learning and solving data.
- **User value:** Preserve personal progress and provide a consistent account across sessions.
- **Priority:** Must-have
- **MVP:** Yes
- **Premium:** No

### 2.2 3×3×3 Beginner Course

- **Description:** Beginner course covering a basic solving method and cube notation.
- **User value:** Provide a structured starting point for learning to solve the Rubik's Cube.
- **Priority:** Must-have
- **MVP:** Yes
- **Premium:** No

### 2.3 3×3×3 CFOP

- **Description:** Course content covering the CFOP advanced solving method.
- **User value:** Help users progress beyond beginner solving.
- **Priority:** Should-have
- **MVP:** Yes / Inc. — original notation retained; exact scope is an open decision.
- **Premium:** No

### 2.4 Other Courses

- **Description:** Additional solving methods and courses for other twisty puzzles.

- **Priority:** Nice-to-have
- **MVP:** No
- **Premium:** TBD

### 2.5 Learning Reminders (Working Title: Clean Reminder)

- **Description:** Reminder functionality intended to encourage users to return to lessons or learning activities.
- **User value:** Support regular learning and practice habits.
- **Priority:** Should-have
- **MVP:** No
- **Premium:** No
- **Open decision:** Confirm the final feature name and whether reminders apply to lessons, training sessions, or both.

### 2.6 Full CFOP

- **Description:** Full CFOP learning content. The initial feature notes associate this item with an approximate sub-20 solving goal.
- **User value:** Support advanced method learning and continued performance improvement.
- **Priority:** Must-have
- **MVP:** TBD

- **Open decision:** Define whether Full CFOP is a separate course, the complete version of the 3×3×3 CFOP course, or a distinct learning tier.

### 2.7 Timer

## 5. Prioritization Principles

Feature prioritization will consider:

- **User value:** The problem the feature solves.
- **Product value:** Its contribution to the overall learning and training experience.
- **Business value:** Its contribution to acquisition, engagement, retention, or monetization.
- **Implementation effort:** Expected development complexity.
- **Dependencies:** Capabilities required before the feature can work.
- **Evidence:** User research and product validation supporting the priority.
- **Release target:** MVP, post-MVP, or a later release.

The current priority classifications are retained as the product's starting point. Any future changes should be based on explicit product decisions and supporting evidence.
